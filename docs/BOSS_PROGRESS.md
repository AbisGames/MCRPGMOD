Frostbound now has a registered entity, unique attributes, independent attacks and phase persistence, craftable sigil, Whitecrown biome gate, transactional altar spawn, boss bar, once-only trophy/Frostcrystal rewards and real server GameTest coverage for attack warning, reload, recipe, summon and drops. Texture/animation states compile against the preserved geometry, but in-client clipping/animation QA remains pending; therefore its visual columns remain partial.

Sköllvar has a separate combat controller for locked scent charges, alternating marked pack pounces and attacks on the last observed footprint. GameTests verify charge avoidance, pounce markers, footprint counterplay and the phase-two third-charge feint.

Update: Sköllvar now has a registered wolf entity, its own attributes and movement, persisted phase/summoning state, client renderer, two phase textures, a survival sigil recipe, Pinewall biome altar route and one-time Wintersteel/trophy rewards. Actual altar summoning and loot pass GameTest. In-entity AI/save-reload and client visual QA still need focused coverage.

Eldaroth now has a registered treant entity, native pose renderer and original geometry preserved, a bark/heartwood atlas, three independent attacks (delayed root lattice with open refuge, fixed seed-pod landings, rotating branch sweep with inner refuge), server damage, saved combat timing and phase-two double-bloom behavior. Its Mooncrystal + Elderwood core + ritual core + oak sapling sigil recipe is registered; the Elderwood altar route and one-time trophy/material rewards are covered by the server encounter test. The current full server run passes all 35 required GameTests, including Eldaroth warning/counterplay/damage, phase reload, exact recipe, biome-bound ritual summon and rewards. Client appearance/clipping remains unverified.

The Fallen King now has a registered king entity with its native sword poses, separate phase textures and the original model geometry retained. Its attacks resolve a locked royal thrust followed by a warned phase-two feint/riposte, heralded parallel phantom-cavalry lanes with a sidestep gap, and three marked regalia sectors plus one safe approach sector. The recipe uses Royal Silver, Heartlands core, ritual core and golden apple; the Heartlands altar encounter and unique trophy/material drop are verified. All 35 server GameTests pass, including fixed aim, warning windows, exposed-sector counterplay and phase-two riposte. Client clipping and animation timing still need in-game QA.

Kragmor is wired as a registered canyon warlord with preserved geometry, native rockfall/tether/leap poses and separate base/awakened textures. Its three arena-safe attacks use locked sequential rock landing shadows, a temporary tether web, then a landing circle followed by a separately warned narrow shock line. Positions and pending timers persist through save/reload; phase two shifts rock lanes. The Redrock Iron + Badlands core + ritual core + pointed dripstone recipe, protected Badlands altar route and unique rewards pass server GameTests. All 35 required tests pass. Client QA remains pending.

Nocthar now has a registered void sovereign, preserved robe/staff geometry, paired base/eclipsed texture sets, fixed-target seam crossings with phase-two topology variation, bounded gravitational pulls with a damage core, and a rotating four-beam eclipse with a separately marked safe corridor. Deep Y bounds (-64 through -1), the special Umbral Sanctuary biome route, Umbrite sigil recipe, once-only trophy and Umbrite reward, and deterministic 20% Voidsteel reward are implemented. All 36 required server GameTests pass, including each counterplay window, phase serialization, exact recipe, deep-Y altar summon, failed-spawn non-consumption, once-only loot and rare drop roll. Client rendering QA remains open.


Aetherion now uses a true flying move controller and 3D path navigation, rises during its altar summon, and keeps its locked dive locations and wake timer through save/reload. Updraft paths lift players before a low sweep, feather links can be interrupted by occupying a node, and the dive circle resolves before a separately warned wake. The high-altitude Aetheris sanctuary route, Y band, exact Skycrystal/core/feather recipe, unique trophy, guaranteed Skycrystal and deterministic 25% Celestium roll pass GameTests. The full server suite passes 37/37 tests and the packaged JAR validator confirms both 256px phase atlases, item assets, recipe and unchanged source geometries. Client appearance QA is pending because the only available Prism client profiles are Minecraft 1.20.1 and a server profile; the mod targets Minecraft 26.1.2.


Vermillion now has independent vessel tethers, three staggered blade orbit heights with a marked center refuge, and three limited spectral court channels that can be interrupted at their source. The phase-two vessel break exposes the monarch to increased incoming damage for a saved 80-tick window. Crimson Wilds biome gating, sigil recipe (Bloodstone + Crimson core + ritual core + Nether Wart), one-time trophy/Bloodstone rewards, and deterministic 25% Bloodcrystal are tested. All 38 required GameTests pass, including attack telegraphs/counterplay, exposure persistence, altar rejection/summoning and rewards. Client appearance QA remains pending.


Longwei now has a segmented jade-dragon renderer and independent serpentine breath, refuge-gap spiral descent and five sequential orb pulses. Phase two adds a separately saved and warned spiral follow-up after the breath. The Jade Empire altar route, exact green-dye recipe, unique trophy and guaranteed Jade Essence rewards pass alongside combat warning/counterplay and reload checks. The complete server suite passes 39/39 tests; the packaged JAR validator confirms retained source geometries, boss/altar classes, textures, models and exact sigil recipes. Client visual QA remains pending because the available Prism profiles do not match Minecraft 26.1.2.


Morgatha now has saved poison-pool bubbles (projectiles can burst a swelling bubble), a slowly pursuing cloud locked to one marked target, and a charged decoy that teleports only the queen before a directional hex wave. Phase two adds a distinct saved 14-tick wave warning. The Marshfen biome altar, exact 5 Mirestone + 2 Marshfen cores + ritual core + slime-ball sigil recipe, guaranteed trophy and eight Mirestone rewards are covered. GameTests verify warnings, avoidance, poison/slow effects, phase-two reload, failed-spawn rollback, and reward consistency. Client appearance QA remains pending because the available Prism profiles do not match Minecraft 26.1.2.


Aurelios now has directional mirror sectors that reduce damage only from guarded angles while announced beams strike along those lines; approaching a mirror core breaks its guard, leaving the open flank exposed. Pressure-grid rows alternate through a marked refuge row. Three treasury orbs travel along announced paths; touching one safely intercepts it and shortens the active mirror shield, while surviving orbs announce their center pulse. Phase two alternates different row lanes and shortens shield duration. Inner Sea summoning, exact Pearlsilver/core/gold recipe, trophy and guaranteed Pearlsilver are covered by GameTests. Client appearance QA remains pending for the unavailable target-version client.


Lianhua now has rotating petal danger arcs around a safe central lily, a fixed coiling river route with a marked opening, and four bloom pulses followed by a separately warned reflected echo. In phase two the petal gap rotates between pulses and the serpent exposes its tail for bonus damage from behind. The Lotus Delta altar, exact Lotus Opal/core/lily-pad sigil recipe, unique trophy and eight guaranteed Lotus Opals are implemented with save/reload and combat counterplay coverage. Client visual QA remains pending for the target Minecraft version.

Akhmar now has a registered Sand Emperor encounter and renderer using the retained source geometry, with separate sandstone and sunfire atlases. The dune procession previews winding lanes and a safe crossing, sun obelisks show stable shadow refuges and can be broken by projectiles, and the buried court locks three sequential eruption silhouettes. Phase two shifts the procession lane, combat clocks and delayed eruptions persist on reload, and the Sunscorch Desert altar uses the exact five Sunglass, two desert core, ritual core, rabbit foot recipe. GameTests cover the attack windows and counterplay, biome-gated summon rollback, trophy and eight Sunglass rewards; target-client visual QA remains pending.

Xaltek now has a registered emerald-jungle encounter using its retained frog-like geometry, native snare/tongue/pounce poses, paired devourer/overgrowth atlases and unique sigil/trophy assets. Players can break the triangular seed pods, interrupt the exposed tongue, or leave the locked canopy landing; breaking all phase-two pods opens a saved weak-point window. The exact Verdantite/emerald-jungle-core recipe, biome-bound altar rollback, trophy/material rewards, combat timing, counterplay and phase save state all pass the 50-test server suite. Client appearance QA remains pending for the target version.

Corallith is a registered coral-isles encounter with retained shell geometry, reef/undertow atlases and a renderer for the shell, sonar and rolling urchin actions. Its broken-ring attack always leaves a safe gate, phase two separately warns and rotates a second sonar sector, and players can shoot the spreading urchins. The exact Tidalite/coral-core recipe, rejected-spawn rollback, trophy/material rewards and attack/reload counterplay pass in the complete 52-test server suite. Client appearance QA remains pending for the target version.
